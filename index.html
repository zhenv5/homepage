I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Pinterest. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at ByteDance AML-US in Seattle. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Srinivasan Parthasarathy.

My research and engineering focus centers on Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning (Federated Learning, Split Learning, Data Clean Rooms), Large-Scale Recommender Systems (Ads ranking, decoupled representation learning, retrieval), Graph Representation Learning, and Large Language Models & Alignment (code generation, proxy fairness). Outside of work, I love vibe coding indie mobile apps, traditional board games, and local-first utilities.
